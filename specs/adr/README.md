# Architecture Decision Records

One decision per file, named `NNNN-short-title.md` (e.g. `0001-use-nixos-wsl.md`), numbered in order and never renumbered.

A superseded ADR is kept and marked `Superseded by NNNN`; it is not deleted or rewritten.

## Template

```markdown
# NNNN. Title

- Status: Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forces are at play and why a decision is needed.

## Decision

What we decided.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result.
```
